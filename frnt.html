ABOUT KARNATAKA

A State Full of Stories

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India known for its rich history, diverse landscapes, vibrant culture, architecture and traditional cuisine.


From royal palaces and ancient temples to waterfalls, forests, beaches and hill stations, Karnataka offers something for every traveller.
